# MM845 · Functional Analysis (IMADA, autumn 2026)

**Status:** preparing for the oral **re-exam**, December 2026 / January 2027.
**Main textbook:** B. MacCluer, *Elementary Functional Analysis* (Springer GTM 253). Supplementary: J. Munkres, *Topology* (for Arzelà–Ascoli / total boundedness), J. B. Conway, *A Course in Functional Analysis*.

🔒 [Course folder · MM845](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1taPH9YO01E31LNqwSHj0FlU1UFHIGJFd)

## Exam format (summary)

- Oral exam. You draw one of **8 topics**, get roughly 30 minutes of preparation, then give a presentation of about 15 minutes.
- The presentation is your own selection of definitions, results and proofs; technical lemmas may be stated without proof to reach the core argument.
- Have a **concrete example** ready for every concept you define — the examiner is likely to ask for one.
- After the presentation: questions on other parts of the curriculum (breadth, not detailed proofs).
- Total around 20–22 minutes, then grading. Bring student ID, driver's licence or passport.

Full topic list with reading map: **[exam-questions.md](exam-questions.md)**.
